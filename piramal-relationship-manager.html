About This Program

A relationship-management certification aligned to Piramal Finance's lending business, covering client acquisition and portfolio servicing. On successful completion, learners are placed into the Relationship Manager role with Piramal Finance, subject to final interview and offer.

  • Official Piramal Finance branded certification on completion
  • Live, instructor-led classes plus on-the-job / branch immersion
  • Dedicated placement desk & mock interview prep
  • Study material, assessments and mentor support included in the fee

    Education
