About This Program

A flagship one-year program with IDFC FIRST Bank that has placed 500+ learners into entry-level banking roles across branches. On successful completion, learners are placed into the Emerging Banker role with IDFC FIRST Bank, subject to final interview and offer.

  • Official IDFC FIRST Bank branded certification on completion
  • Live, instructor-led classes plus on-the-job / branch immersion
  • Dedicated placement desk & mock interview prep
  • Study material, assessments and mentor support included in the fee
