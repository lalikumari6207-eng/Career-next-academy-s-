How Registration & Enrollment Works

1

Register (₹249, one-time)

Fill your personal & education details, verify your mobile number with OTP, and pay a one-time ₹249 registration fee. You'll instantly get a CareerNext Registration Number (e.g. CNA-2026-000000) — save it.

2

Choose Your Program

Using your Registration Number, pick any one of our Banking, Insurance & Finance, Fellowship or Leadership programs from the Registration page.

3

Pay Full or in Installments

Your registration fee is adjusted into your program fee. Pay the full amount up front, or split it into installments (2–5, depending on the program) shown on the Registration page.

4

Train & Get Placed

Attend classes/on-the-job training for your program's duration, receive your stipend (where applicable) from month two, and get placed with our employer partner on successful completion.
