About This Program

A fast-track certification for the Gold Loan business, ending in a 2-year fixed-term associate placement with Kotak Mahindra Bank's Gold Loan vertical. On successful completion, learners are placed into the Gold Loan Relationship Officer role with Kotak Mahindra Bank, subject to final interview and offer.

  • Official Kotak Mahindra Bank branded certification on completion
  • Live, instructor-led classes plus on-the-job / branch immersion
  • Dedicated placement desk & mock interview prep
  • Study material, assessments and mentor support included in the fee

    • Stipend During Training

    • Monthly stipend (estimated)₹8,000 – ₹10,000 / month
    • Paid fromMonth 2 of training onward*
    • Post-training starting CTC₹3 LPA Fixed (2-year fixed-term associate contract) + Performance Linked Pay

    *Stipend figures are CareerNext Academy estimates for this program tier — actual amount is confirmed with Kotak Mahindra Bank at induction, not a figure published by Kotak Mahindra Bank itself.
