Our Story

Founded 2016. Launched 2017.

CareerNext Academy began in 2016 with one clear vision — to turn graduates into job-ready professionals, not just degree holders. In 2017 we launched our very first training batch in Banking & NBFC careers, and have since grown into India's trusted training-to-placement partner across Banking, Insurance, Finance and Leadership.

2016

Founded

CareerNext Academy was founded with a single goal: bridge the gap between classroom degrees and real industry-ready skills.

2017

Launched

Our first job-ready training batch went live, placing graduates directly into Banking & NBFC roles with leading recruiter partners.

Today

25,000+ Careers Built

From one batch to a nationwide training-to-placement platform, trusted by 500+ recruiter partners across India.
