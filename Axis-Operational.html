About This Program

A 1-year operations training pipeline for Axis Bank. You learn cash handling, deposits and withdrawals, account opening, KYC and branch operations. On successful completion, learners are placed into the Assistant Manager (Cashier) role with Axis Bank, subject to final interview and offer.

  • Axis Bank Operational Programme certification on completion
  • Live classes on cash handling, counter operations, KYC and banking software, plus branch immersion
  • Dedicated placement desk & mock interview prep
  • Study material, assessments and mentor support included in the fee
